## Intelligence evaluation

**Answer Readiness — 3/5 (60).** `/pricing` and `/` carry real FAQ blocks with FAQPage schema, and
`/scope` answers "what can it actually do" directly. But most sections open with a marketing line,
not with the answer, and no page is built around a question a user would type.
*Key finding:* FAQs exist, but sections still lead with pitch, not answer.

**Quotability — 3/5 (60).** The pricing comparison table, the fits/cannot-do lists on `/scope` and
the numbered legal clauses extract cleanly. Elsewhere the copy is short paragraphs that depend on
the surrounding page.
*Key finding:* Tables and lists are quotable; prose paragraphs are not self-contained.

**Evidence Density — 2/5 (40).** Plenty of product numbers (prices, 5/10/20 min footage caps, GB
quotas, 1080×1920), but no measurements, no sources, no author and nothing verifiable from outside.
*Key finding:* Product numbers only — no data, sources or attribution anywhere.

**Content Depth — 2/5 (40).** Visible body text runs ~80–215 words per page; eight pages in total.
There is no documentation, no guide, no worked example an agent could lean on.
*Key finding:* Eight thin pages; no docs or how-to corpus to cite.

**Freshness — 3/5 (60).** `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/pricing` carry a visible 2026-09-22 update date;
the rest have no date signal and there is no feed, so nothing announces change.
*Key finding:* Dates on three pages, none elsewhere, no feed.

**Structural Clarity — 4/5 (80).** One H1 per page, a clean H2/H3 outline, semantic markup, and the
content is in the HTML (no JS needed). `/login` is the only page with a weak outline.
*Key finding:* Clean, server-rendered HTML with a readable heading outline.

Intelligence = average(3,3,2,2,3,4) × 20 = **57/100**.

## Prioritized fixes

1. **Write answer-first pages for real questions** (high impact / medium effort).
   One page per question people actually type: "ตัดคลิป TikTok ด้วย AI ยังไง", "ใส่ซับไทยอัตโนมัติ",
   "ตัดคลิปรีวิวสินค้าให้เร็วขึ้น", "AI ตัดต่อวิดีโอภาษาไทยตัวไหนดี". Each answers in the first
   paragraph (40–60 words, self-contained), then the detail. This lifts Answer Readiness, Quotability
   and Content Depth at once.
2. **Add a docs / knowledge section** (high impact / medium effort). Modes, limits, file support,
   what the AI does and does not do, troubleshooting. A stable corpus is what an agent quotes when
   asked "how does X work".
3. **Put evidence in the copy** (medium impact / low effort). Measured numbers the product already
   has — how long a 5-minute clip takes, how much time the draft saves, what the render outputs —
   with dates. Add an author/team line and a "last updated" date to every page, not just legal ones.
4. **Add an RSS/Atom feed** (medium impact / low effort). Closes a failing check and gives AI
   systems a change signal. Needs the content from fix 1 to be worth anything.
5. **Submit to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then earn a few links.** Nothing on
   this page is discoverable until the site is in an index that assistants read.

## Recommendation

The foundation is already better than most sites: crawlable, structured, `llms.txt` in place. The
gap is content — eight short pages give an assistant nothing to quote. Writing four answer-first
pages plus a small docs section is the single change that moves this from B- to A-, and it matters
more than the custom domain. Re-run this audit after those land.

### What changed

- Six new pages under `/guide` (five answer-first articles plus a help/docs page) and a
  `/guide` index, all registered in `src/lib/site.ts` so the sitemap, metadata, feed and
  tests follow automatically.
- A visible "อัปเดตล่าสุด" date and a team author line on every content page, from the same
  registry entry that feeds the sitemap and JSON-LD.
- `/feed.xml` (Atom), `.md` twins for `/scope` and every guide page, and an extended
  `llms.txt` (answered-questions list, guide links, a declared-limits section, feed link).
- New JSON-LD: `Article` per guide page, `HowTo` for the home page's three steps, `ItemList`
  for the guide index, and `FAQPage` added to `/scope` and `/about`.

### Known gaps

- `answerFirstRatio` stays 0% — that heuristic matches English copulas ("is", "are",
  "means"), which Thai copy cannot satisfy. It is a scorer limitation, not a content one.
- `/login` and `/signup` remain below the 250-word depth threshold. They are conversion
  pages; padding them would be noise. `/login` is `noindex` by design.
- `/privacy` is 190 words. Legal text is not padded for a crawler.
- No measured numbers (render time, time saved, transcription accuracy, user counts) appear
  anywhere. The placeholders live in `src/lib/owner-numbers.ts` and render as nothing until
  the owner measures them.
